# NovaBot Cognitive Architecture (AI System)

## 🧠 Overview
NovaBot does not use a "black box" AI. Instead, it employs a **specialized Tri-Modal Cognitive Architecture**. The AI is not asked to "trade everything"; it is asked specific questions depending on the context.

## 🤖 The "Three Brains" System
The AI logic is split into three distinct phases, each with its own specific prompt and goal:

### 1. The Strategist (`analyze_market`)
- **Role**: Macro-analyst.
- **Goal**: Determine if the market is safe for trading.
- **Input**: 15m/1h/4h Trends, Bitcoin Dominance, Funding Rates.
- **Output**: Market Bias (BULLISH/BEARISH/NEUTRAL) and Volatility Assessment.
- **Impact**: If the Strategist says "Extreme Danger", the bot will **NOT** even look for signals.

### 2. The Gatekeeper (`validate_signal`)
- **Role**: Risk Officer.
- **Goal**: Validate specific entry signals generated by the math algorithms.
- **Input**: Technical Signal (e.g., "Buy BTC, Price=98000"), current RSI/ADX/EMA.
- **Persona Injection**: This brain adopts a specific **Persona** (e.g., "Sniper" or "Aggressive Scalper") defined in the strategy file.
  - *Example*: A "Sniper" persona will reject a signal if the trend is weak, even if the math signal is valid.
- **Output**: `CONFIRMED` or `REJECTED` (with reasoning).

### 3. The Monitor (`analyze_active_position`)
- **Role**: Portfolio Manager.
- **Goal**: Manage open risk.
- **Input**: Current PnL, Time in Trade, Distance to Stop Loss.
- **Output**: suggestions (Hold, Close,Tighten Stop).

---

## 🎭 Dynamic Persona System
The AI changes its behavior based on the `AI_PERSONA` setting in the strategy files.

| Persona | Behavior | Risk Tolerance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sniper** | Extremely picky. Waits for perfect confluence. | Low |
| **Conservative Scalper** | Quick in/out. Hates drawdowns. | Low-Medium |
| **Aggressive Day Trader** | Follows momentum. Tolerates volatility. | High |
| **Liquidity Hunter** | Contrarian. Buys crashes if structure holds. | High (Calculated) |

## 🔌 Technical Implementation
- **Provider**: OpenRouter (giving access to Clause 3.5 Sonnet, GPT-4o, etc.).
- **Resilience**:
  - **Circuit Breaker**: Stops AI calls if API fails repeatedly.
  - **Caching**: Analyses are cached (Market: 15m, Position: 5m) to save costs and speed up response.
  - **Fallback**: If AI is down, the bot falls back to `Hard Veto` rules only.
